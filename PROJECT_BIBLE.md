# PROJECT BIBLE
### ENDWORLDS: THE LONG WAY HOME

> **Purpose:** the single canon reference for story, characters, cosmology and tone.
> **Status legend:** **CANON** = established fact (owner-approved; change only with owner approval) · **PROPOSAL** = suggestion awaiting owner decision · **TBD** = intentionally undefined — a known gap.

This document only states what is established by the master instructions ([AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)) and marks the rest as TBD or PROPOSAL. Nothing here invents lore.

---

## 1. Premise — CANON

A young Zambian man, heartbroken at the story's opening, becomes stranded across **real parallel universes** when an experimental VR headset malfunctions. His journey home becomes something larger: rediscovering himself, helping others across the Endworlds, and building a new home. The central mystery involves **alternate versions of the protagonist** scattered across the multiverse.

**Logline (working):** *Stranded between worlds by a broken headset, a young man searches for home — and finds that home is something you build, not somewhere you return to.*

## 2. Themes — CANON (direction), TBD (expression)

Identity · loss · discovery · parallel universes · the meaning of home. NARRATIVE RULE: the story must **not** be reduced to winning back the former love (AGENTS.md rule 3). Heartbreak is the inciting wound, not the destination.

## 3. The Protagonist — CANON

- Young Zambian man, **approximately 19–23 years old**.
- Appearance is **fixed and consistent** across all artwork, animations, illustrations and scenes; the **approved character reference is the definitive visual source** (reference asset: TBD — none registered in the manifest yet).
- Zambian identity is expressed authentically and respectfully through character, perspective, personal details and cultural context — **never through stereotypes**.

- Alternate versions of the protagonist are **central to the long-term mystery**.

## 5. The multiverse — CANON rules

Travel and cosmology mechanics (shared with [GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md](GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md); the bible owns the *fiction*, the GDD owns the *systems*):

| Element | Canon definition |
|---|---|
| **The headset** | An experimental VR headset whose malfunction makes travel physically real. It is the protagonist's constant companion and primary interface with the multiverse. |
| **Random Jump** | Uncontrolled travel between universes. Worlds encountered this way are real, persistent places. |
| **Beacon Travel** | Registered beacons allow deliberate return to a universe. Not every visited world has one. |
| **Home Signal** | The long-term objective: a way to locate (or define) home. The endgame axis of the story. |
| **Dimensional Recall** | Increases the chance of revisiting previously encountered but unregistered worlds — **never guarantees** a specific destination. |
| **Forgotten worlds** | Worlds without beacons remain eligible for random encounters. Nothing visited is truly lost. |
| **World persistence** | The state of a world persists **independently of beacon registration**. Consequences stick. |
| **The Sanctuary** | An expandable home between universes; the literal construction of "home" as a theme. |
| **The Traveller's Journal** | In-fiction and mechanical record of discoveries, lore, relationships and the protagonist's personal development. |

World naming, universe count and the nature of the "Endworlds" themselves: **TBD / PROPOSAL space.**

## 6. Tone — CANON (direction)

Emotionally engaging, expansive, distinctive. Melancholy sits alongside wonder; the game treats its protagonist's interior life seriously. Quality and meaningful exploration take priority over volume of content (AGENTS.md closing statement).

## 7. Canon control

1. Only the project owner approves canon. Contributors write new material as **PROPOSAL** blocks or in clearly-marked proposal sections.
2. Canon changes happen through pull requests against this file (and related docs), with owner review — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. When new canon is approved, update this file, the GDD, and any affected manifests **in the same PR** (rule 9).
